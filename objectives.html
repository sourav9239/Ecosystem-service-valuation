Primary Objective

This study analyzes how Ecosystem Service Values (ESV) change when natural and semi-natural lands are converted into urban areas. By mapping land use shifts over time, it quantifies the resulting loss or gain in benefits like clean water, air, and recreation. The goal is to understand the direct impact of city growth on crucial environmental services. This research is vital for sustainable urban planning and conservation efforts.

Specific Objectives

  • To study the impacts of urban expansion over peripheral land use.
  • To analyse the variations in ESV as the function of urban expansion.
  • To identify the ecologically vulnerable metropolis in regard to ESV.
